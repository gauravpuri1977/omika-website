PRODUCTION READINESS
This is a front-end prototype, not a live ecommerce backend. Real catalogue data, prices, variants, inventory, cart/checkout, payment gateway, shipping, customer accounts, newsletter capture and approved legal policies must be integrated before accepting orders. Payment marks are placeholders pending gateway confirmation. The sample page uses noindex,follow while it remains a preview route.
